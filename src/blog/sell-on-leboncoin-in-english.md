Leboncoin is where France sells its stuff: 30 million visitors a month, by Leboncoin's own count (March 2026). Sofas, prams, bikes, jackets, phones. Whatever is gathering dust in your flat, someone a few streets away is searching for it.

For English speakers, there are two hurdles. Leboncoin has no English version, and your listing has to be written in French. Neither is a dealbreaker. This guide covers everything you need to sell there from France — account, rules, payment, delivery, safety — checked against Leboncoin's help centre on 9 October 2026. Then the shortcut: an app in English that turns one photo into a Leboncoin listing written in French.

## The short version

- **No English Leboncoin.** The site, the app and the help centre are in French only. Your browser can translate the pages so you can read them; your listing still has to be written in French.
- **You need to be in France.** From abroad, Leboncoin blocks posting a listing and buying with secure payment, and its messaging generally works only for conversations started from France.
- **You need the right mobile number.** French, or from an EU country, Switzerland, Liechtenstein, Monaco or a French overseas territory. UK and US numbers are not on Leboncoin's list.
- **Selling costs you nothing in most categories.** Posting is free for private sellers outside some Vehicles and Real estate subcategories, and secure payment is free for the seller: the buyer pays the fees.
- **The shortcut:** FillSell's Lens writes your Leboncoin listing in French from a photo, inside an app that speaks English. The Chrome extension then posts it from your own Leboncoin session, and the same item can go out on Vinted, Leboncoin, eBay, Beebs, and Depop.

## Is there an English version of Leboncoin?

No. We checked three ways on 9 October 2026:

| Where | Language | What it means for you |
|---|---|---|
| The Leboncoin app (App Store) | French only | Menus, buttons and notifications in French |
| The help centre (assistance.leboncoin.info) | French only | Rules and how-tos in French |
| Leboncoin's own words | “not designed for international use” | No English mode is coming to the rescue |

For comparison, Vinted's app lists 21 languages on the App Store, English included.

So how do English speakers cope? On a computer, your browser's built-in translation turns Leboncoin's pages into readable English (in Chrome: right-click, then “Translate to English”). That is enough to browse, read the help centre and follow a conversation. It doesn't fix the hard part: writing. Leboncoin's rules say every listing must be written in French — a legal obligation, Leboncoin says, under the French language law of 4 August 1994. A listing in English breaks the rules, however good the photos.

## What you need before your first listing

| You need | What Leboncoin's help says | Expat tip |
|---|---|---|
| A Leboncoin account | Free, and mandatory to buy or sell. Sign up with your email, “Continuer avec Google” or “Continuer avec Apple” (Apple isn't offered on Android). | Google or Apple sign-in skips the password step. |
| A mobile number | Mandatory, checked by a code sent by SMS. French numbers are accepted, plus numbers from every EU country, Switzerland, Liechtenstein, Monaco and the French overseas territories. One number, one account. | UK, US, Canadian and Australian numbers are not on the list: get a French or EU SIM first. |
| To be in France | Posting a listing is not possible from abroad. | Leaving France for good? Post before you go. |
| An IBAN | Needed to receive your sales on your bank account. You can add it after your first listing: Mon compte, Informations personnelles, Moyens de paiement. IBANs linked to a Trade Republic account are not accepted. | Add it early, so your first sale isn't a scramble. |
| An ID document | Above a certain amount of transactions, Leboncoin asks for an ID card or a passport: valid, in colour, sharp, all four corners visible. | Leboncoin names the passport alongside the ID card. |

**Selling a lot, or buying to resell?** Leboncoin is clear: if you buy items in order to resell them, regularly and in volume, you need a Pro account — free to create, and mandatory for professionals. Auto-entrepreneurs go through a Pro account too. It changes the rules: no secure payment with a hand-over in person, and new products from professionals are refused in categories such as Mode and Maison.

**Tax.** Under the EU's DAC7 reporting rules, Leboncoin passes your transaction history to the French tax authorities once you reach 30 completed transactions or €2,000 in a calendar year. Leboncoin adds that this does not necessarily mean you owe tax.

## The rules your Leboncoin listing must follow

Leboncoin checks listings and refuses the ones that break its rules. The ones that trip up newcomers:

1. **Written in French.** Title and description, no exceptions.
2. **One listing per item.** You can't post the same ad twice: each listing needs its own title, description and photos, and a photo can't already be used in another of your listings.
3. **Located where the item is.** Your listing sits in the commune where the item is, even if you offer delivery across France.
4. **No contact details.** No phone number or email address in the title, the description or a photo.
5. **No links.** Nothing that sends buyers to a site other than leboncoin.fr — so no “see my other shop”.
6. **Right category, first time.** The category can't be changed after posting: you would have to delete the listing and post it again.
7. **Your own photos.** Up to 20 free photos in Mode, Famille, Maison et jardin, Électronique and Loisirs. Photos must be yours or royalty-free; pictures with a link, a QR code or contact details are refused.

How long does a listing stay online? It depends on the category. Leboncoin's help says some categories, Mode included, have no time limit, and the exact duration shows when you post. When a listing expires, the “Prolonger” button (or the renewal email) extends it for free outside Vehicles and Real estate.

## Step by step: your first sale on Leboncoin

1. **Create your account.** “Se connecter”, top right of the home page, then your email, Google or Apple, and the SMS code.
2. **Shoot good photos.** Daylight, a plain background, the label, any flaw.
3. **Write in French.** A clear title (item, brand, size, colour) and an honest description of the condition.
4. **Post.** “Déposer une annonce”: category, photos, title, description, price, location.
5. **Choose how it changes hands.** Delivery, a hand-over in person, or both. Secure payment comes switched on by default with delivery.
6. **Answer your messages.** Buyers write in Leboncoin's messaging, in French.
7. **Confirm availability, fast.** Once a buyer has paid, you have 48 hours to confirm the item is available, or the sale is cancelled automatically.
8. **Ship or meet.** The buyer then has 3 days to confirm everything is as described.
9. **Get paid.** The money lands in your Leboncoin wallet. Transfer it to your bank account whenever you like.

## Getting paid: secure payment, delivery or hand-over

Secure payment (“Transaction sécurisée”, also called “paiement sécurisé”) is the backbone of selling on Leboncoin. For the seller, it is free.

| | With delivery | Hand-over in person |
|---|---|---|
| The seller pays | Nothing | Nothing |
| The buyer pays in fees | €0.70 + 5 % of the price | €1.99 at most with a private seller |
| Example: a €40 jacket | €2.70 in fees, plus postage | €1.99 at most |
| You receive | €40 | €40 |

With a hand-over, the buyer pays online, you confirm availability within 48 hours, you agree on a place and time in the messaging, and on the day the buyer checks the item and releases the payment from the app. You can also untick delivery and keep the hand-over only, cash included — but Leboncoin states that it guarantees nothing done outside its messaging and secure payment.

With delivery, the buyer pays the postage and picks a carrier among the ones you allow:

| Carrier | Highest item price | Highest weight |
|---|---|---|
| Mondial Relay (relay point) | €2,000 | 25 kg |
| Shop2Shop by Chronopost | €2,000 | 20 kg |
| Colissimo | €400 | 30 kg at a drop-off point, 5 kg from a letterbox |
| Courrier suivi | €400 | 2 kg, 3 cm thick |

A concrete case: you sell a pram for €450. Colissimo and Courrier suivi are out, because of their €400 ceiling. Mondial Relay or Shop2Shop by Chronopost can take it if it fits their size and weight limits. Otherwise, a hand-over does the job. Limits come from Leboncoin's help and can change: check them when you post.

## Staying safe: Leboncoin's own anti-scam rules

When the language isn't yours, a scam is harder to spot. Leboncoin's help centre lists the warning signs:

- Keep every exchange in Leboncoin's secure messaging, and every payment in secure payment.
- Never give a buyer a verification code, your card number or your ID documents.
- Be wary of external links, urgent requests for money and messages that never mention your item.
- Leboncoin will never ask you to validate a payment through a QR code in the messaging.
- An email says you've been paid, but nothing shows in the messaging? Don't ship anything.
- Leboncoin never phones you about a blocked account.

Leboncoin even counts spelling mistakes among the signs of a suspicious message. Worth remembering when you write your own listing.
