## What is crosslisting?

Crosslisting means putting the same item up for sale on several second-hand marketplaces at once. More sites, more buyers who see it. Done by hand, it's a grind: retype the title, re-upload the photos, rewrite the description site by site, then race to take the other listings down when the item sells.

With FillSell you write it once — and Lens does the writing. Each marketplace gets a listing shaped to its own fields, your stock stays up to date in one tap, and a recorded sale takes the item's other listings down.

## How do you sell the same item on Vinted and Leboncoin at the same time?

Create the listing once, tick Vinted and Leboncoin (plus any of your other marketplaces), and the Chrome extension posts each version from your computer, one listing after another, at a human pace. The item is then live on both sites, each with a listing in its own format — in French, as the French sites expect.

The day it sells on Vinted, FillSell records the sale on its own and removes the Leboncoin listing. If it sells on Leboncoin, you confirm the sale with one tap, and the Vinted listing comes down in turn. If there's any doubt, the “Already sold?” question comes first. Step by step: [crosslisting Vinted and Leboncoin in English](page:crosslisting/vinted-leboncoin), [selling the same item on several marketplaces without double-selling (in French)](page:fr:blog/vendre-meme-article-vinted-leboncoin-ebay-beebs), and if Leboncoin is new to you, [how to sell on Leboncoin in English](page:blog/sell-on-leboncoin-in-english).

## Your phone runs the show, your computer does the work

That's FillSell's edge. On your phone (iPhone or Android) or at fillsell.app, you snap, tick and confirm — at the flea market, on the train, from the sofa. On your computer, the Chrome extension does the legwork on the sites: it fills in the forms, reads your listings, spots your sales, removes and reposts, one listing at a time, in a minimised window that never takes over your screen.

It works in your own session, the one you're already signed in to. FillSell never asks for your Vinted, Leboncoin, Beebs or Depop passwords; for Depop, you allow access with one click in the extension. Computer off? Nothing is lost: your listings wait and go out the next time Chrome opens. And on eBay France, once your account is linked on eBay's official page, your listings go through [eBay's official API](page:plateformes/ebay), even with your computer off.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is a crosslisting app?

A crosslisting app puts the same item up for sale on several marketplaces from a single card, then helps you keep track of those listings. FillSell goes all the way: Lens writes the listing from a photo, syncing brings in the stock you already have live, and the item's other listings are removed once its sale is recorded.

### Which marketplaces and countries does FillSell work with?

Today, {{plateformes}}, with accounts based in France (vinted.fr for Vinted, ebay.fr for eBay). You tick, item by item, where each listing should go. Vinted UK, eBay.co.uk and eBay.com aren't covered today. FillSell is not affiliated with any of these marketplaces.

### Is FillSell available in English?

Yes: the app on iPhone, Android and the web is in English and French, and Lens drafts your listings in French for the French sites. The Chrome extension and its store page are in French for now.

### Do I need to keep my computer on?

For the extension to do its work (publishing, reposting, spotting your sales, removing listings), your computer must be on with Chrome open; while listings are queued, the extension keeps it from going to sleep. Computer off? Nothing is lost: everything waits and goes out the next time Chrome opens. The exception: on eBay France, once your account is linked, your listings go through eBay's official API, even with your computer off.

### Does FillSell ask for my passwords?

Never. You stay signed in to your accounts in your browser as usual, and the extension works within those sessions. For Depop, you allow access with one click in the extension. For eBay, you link your account on eBay's official page: FillSell never sees your password.

### Can I bring in the listings I already have live?

Yes. One tap on Sync and your listings already live on {{plateformes}} land in your stock, neatly filed, free and with no limit. Syncing reads your listings: nothing is published or edited. The same item on several marketplaces becomes one card when its photos and title match; when in doubt, FillSell asks you.

### What happens when an item sells?

When Vinted or eBay marks your item as sold, FillSell records the sale on its own. On Leboncoin and Beebs, it asks you to confirm with one tap. Once the sale is recorded, the item's other listings are removed. If FillSell has any doubt about a listing, it asks you “Already sold?” before touching it.

Your plan counts the items written by AI; once written, an item goes out to all your marketplaces at no extra count. And your stock has no item limit, even on the free plan.
