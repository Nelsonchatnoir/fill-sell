## Why plug your Vinted into FillSell?

Vinted is where second-hand fashion lives. But a listing that only exists on Vinted only ever meets Vinted's buyers. With FillSell, your wardrobe becomes a stock: one card per item, listed wherever you decide among {{plateformes}}, tracked all the way to the sale.

The split of roles is what makes it work:

- **Your phone runs the show.** You take the photo, read the listing, tick your marketplaces and follow your sales. In the app, wherever you are.
- **Your computer does the work.** The FillSell Chrome extension works on Vinted in your session: it fills in Vinted's forms, one listing after another.
- **You stay in charge.** Any doubt, FillSell asks you instead of guessing.

One listing, {{nb_plateformes}} marketplaces. Nothing to retype from what you already have on Vinted. And listings that climb back up, with one tap or, depending on your subscription, in the time slot you chose.

## Publish: one card, a listing cut for Vinted

It starts with a photo. Lens, FillSell's AI ([how it works, in French](page:fr:fonctions/lens)), recognises the item, reads the brand when it's written on the item, suggests a condition (from “New with tags” to “Satisfactory”) and writes the title and description. The suggested price? Lens looks for comparable listings on Vinted first, then on eBay and Leboncoin, and tells you how many listings it's based on. You read it, fix it, approve it.

Then each marketplace gets its own version. For Vinted: the right spot in Vinted's category tree, the size, the colour, the parcel size. A detail missing? A category nowhere to be found? The app asks you, with ready-made choices. Never a reminder on a loop, never a marketplace quietly greyed out.

Working in English? The app is available in English and French, and the listing headed for vinted.fr is drafted in French, the language Vinted France's buyers search in. You read every version before it goes out.

The posting happens on your computer: the extension fills in Vinted's form, listing after listing, at a human pace. A whole batch? Pick the items in your stock, tick the marketplaces, the app prepares everything, you check, off it goes.

One habit to keep: on Vinted, photos show the item as it is. Post them as they are.

## Sync: your whole Vinted wardrobe, filed in one tap

Already selling on Vinted? Don't retype a thing. Tap Sync: the extension reads the Vinted listings you already have live, and they land in your FillSell stock, filed alongside those from your other marketplaces. Free, with no item limit. Nothing is published, nothing is edited.

An item live on Vinted and somewhere else becomes a single card. FillSell compares the photos AND the title, never one without the other. Whenever it's unsure, the question “Is it the same item?” waits for you in a separate list of listings to check, outside your stock. And two Vinted listings are two units: FillSell never merges one into the other.

## Repost: bump your Vinted listings back up

Why repost (or relist, as many sellers say)? Because on Vinted, a listing's age counts. How long ago an item was uploaded is one of the ranking factors Vinted publishes, and Vinted says it promotes new listings ([recommended content](https://www.vinted.ie/help/409)). A listing that gets older slides down. Reposting sends it back to the top.

Reposting means taking the listing down and putting it back online. Let's be straight with you: it isn't Vinted's paid Bump. The new listing climbs back to the top, but its views and favourites start from zero. Better you hear it from us.

Two ways to do it:

- **With one tap.** Pick a listing, tap, and your computer reposts it; several at once depending on your subscription, reposted one by one.
- **Automatically, depending on your subscription.** Your listings bump themselves back up on {{republication}}. You choose the days, the time slot (morning, noon, evening or custom) and the age from which a listing goes back up, never less than a week. Computer on, Chrome open, and you get on with something else.

## Sell: Vinted says “sold”, FillSell records it and takes the copies down

This is where it all happens. When Vinted marks your item sold, FillSell records the sale on its own, on proof: your Vinted wardrobe or the listing page read as sold. Then the item's other listings are removed from your other marketplaces, as long as a proof links them to it: posted by FillSell, same identifier, imported, linked by you, or identical photo.

A copy FillSell isn't sure about? No removal in the dark. FillSell asks you “Already sold?”, and you decide: “Yes, remove it” or “No, it's another copy”.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is FillSell a Vinted extension or an app?

Both, each with its own role. The FillSell app (iPhone, Android, and the web at fillsell.app) is where you create your listings, run your stock and follow your sales. The Chrome extension, installed on your computer, does the work on Vinted: it fills in the forms, reads your listings, spots your sales, removes and reposts, inside your session.

### Does FillSell need my Vinted password?

No, never. You sign in to Vinted in Chrome as usual, and the extension works inside that open session. FillSell neither reads nor stores your Vinted password.

### Does my computer have to stay on?

For Vinted, yes: posting, reposting, removing a listing and spotting a sale all need your computer on, Chrome open and your Vinted session active. While listings are queued, the extension keeps the computer awake. Computer off, nothing is lost: everything waits for the next time Chrome opens.

### How do I bump a Vinted listing with FillSell?

By reposting it: the extension takes it down and puts it back online, and it starts again from the top of the results, with views and favourites reset to zero. Do it with one tap or, depending on your subscription, let automatic reposting take care of it on {{republication}}, on the days and in the time slot you choose. It isn't a Bump: it's a new listing.

### What happens when my item sells on Vinted?

When Vinted marks the item sold, FillSell records the sale on its own, then the item's listings linked by a proof are removed from your other marketplaces. For an uncertain copy, FillSell asks you “Already sold?” before touching it. Once the sale is recorded, you also get an email.

### Is it risky for my Vinted account?

No tool can guarantee an account will never be restricted, and FillSell doesn't promise that. Since summer 2026, sellers have reported 24-hour restrictions for “automated activity”; Vinted hasn't published any statement about it. FillSell plays it safe: it acts in your session, one listing after another, at a human pace, pauses automatic reposting on Vinted while Vinted is checking your account, and you switch everything off in one tap. Vinted's rules are in section 6 of its [terms and conditions](https://www.vinted.ie/terms-and-conditions); our [account safety page (in French)](page:fr:securite-des-comptes) quotes them word for word.

### Does FillSell work with Vinted UK, Ireland or other Vinted sites?

Not today: FillSell works with Vinted France, on vinted.fr. Vinted's other sites (vinted.co.uk, vinted.ie, vinted.be, vinted.es, vinted.it…) aren't covered today.

### How much does FillSell cost for selling on Vinted?

You start for free (€0), no credit card; Premium is €12.99, Pro €29.99 and Business €59.99 a month, no commitment. The Chrome extension is free, and so is syncing. On Vinted's side, selling costs a private seller nothing: buyers pay the Vinted fee. Every plan in detail: [FillSell pricing](page:tarifs).
