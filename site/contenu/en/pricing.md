No credit card to get started. No contract to keep going. No surcharge per marketplace. You start at €0, step up when your sales call for it, and stop whenever you want.

## What every plan includes, Free included

The Free plan isn't a demo that switches itself off after a fortnight. It's the real FillSell: the whole crosslisting core, plus a monthly batch of AI-written listings to get you going.

### One photo, and the listing writes itself

Snap the item: Lens, the AI that writes your listings ([how it works, in French](page:fr:fonctions/lens)), recognises it, reads the brand, suggests a condition and a price drawn from comparable listings found online, then writes the title and description. You read it, fix what you want, publish. Each scan counts as one listing on your plan.

### One listing, every marketplace you sell on

Fill in the listing once and tick where it goes: {{plateformes}}. Each marketplace gets a version cut to its own fields (category, size, colour, parcel); if a detail is missing, the app asks you instead of guessing. The Chrome extension then posts your listings from your computer, one by one, at a human pace. And on eBay France, once [your eBay account is linked, your listings go out through eBay's official API](page:plateformes/ebay), even with your computer off.

### Your live stock, filed in one tap

One tap on Sync and the listings you already have live land in your stock, neatly filed: a single card per item, even when it's for sale on several marketplaces. FillSell compares the photos and the title; whenever it isn't sure, it asks you rather than deciding on its own. Syncing is free, with no limit, whatever your plan.

### Sold here, removed there

When Vinted or eBay marks your item sold, FillSell records the sale on its own. On Leboncoin and Beebs, it asks you to confirm with one tap. As soon as the sale is recorded, FillSell removes the item's other listings; if one of them looks uncertain, it asks you "Already sold?" before touching it. All of that on the Free plan: no top-tier plan needed to [avoid selling the same item twice (in French)](page:fr:blog/vendre-meme-article-vinted-leboncoin-ebay-beebs).

### Your stock, your sales, your margin

Your stock has no item limit, even on the free plan. Sales, fees, margin and profit work themselves out; an item whose purchase price you don't know is left out of the maths rather than counted as free, so your margin stays real. Export your stock and sales to Excel, or import your own Excel or CSV file. ### A listing back on top, in one tap

On every plan, you bump a listing with one tap: your computer takes it down and puts it back online, and it starts again from the top of the results. Its views and favourites start from zero: that's how reposting works.

### Your voice, your phone, your computer

Dictate an item or manage your stock by voice. The app is free on the App Store (iPhone and iPad, iOS 15 or later) and on Google Play (Android 7 or later), and it also opens at fillsell.app on your computer, in English or French. The [Chrome extension](/extension) installs for free, once, and updates itself.

Your phone gives the orders, your computer does the legwork: the extension works inside your own sessions, and FillSell never asks for your marketplace passwords. For listings to go out on the sites where the extension works, your computer needs to be on with Chrome open. Computer off? Nothing is lost: everything waits and goes out the next time Chrome opens.

## What you pay for with Premium, Pro or Business

### You pay for written listings, never for posts

Your plan counts the items written by AI; once written, an item goes out to all your marketplaces at no extra count. So you never pay per marketplace, and never per post. Premium, Pro and Business give you far more written listings each month than Free: enough to sell every week, then at high volume, then to make it your business. The counter resets every month, on the anniversary date of your subscription (of your sign-up on the Free plan).

### Photos that pop

With Premium, Pro or Business, AI touches up your photos (lighting, background): a light touch-up, or an advanced one with the background of your choice. When you publish, you decide: touched-up photos or as they are.

## How do you pay, and how do you stop?

- **On the web**: you subscribe at fillsell.app, and payment is handled securely by Stripe.
- **On iPhone and Android**: the subscription goes through the App Store or Google Play, like any in-app purchase.
- **The price that counts** is the one shown when you buy; store prices can differ from the website's. Prices are in euros.
- **Stop whenever you want**: your subscription runs until the end of the paid period, with no pro-rata refund for the days left. Subscribed on the website? Cancel from the app. Subscribed through the App Store or Google Play? The app opens the store's subscription page for you.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is FillSell free?

Yes, to start. The Free plan costs €0, opens without a credit card and has no end date. It includes Lens, publishing on {{plateformes}}, syncing with no limit, removing an item's other listings after a sale, a stock with no item limit and margin tracking, with a monthly batch of AI-written listings. The iPhone and Android apps, the web app and the Chrome extension are free too. To write more listings, move up to Premium, Pro or Business.

### How much is FillSell Premium?

€12.99 a month, no commitment. On top of Free, Premium brings far more AI-written listings each month, AI touch-ups for your photos (lighting, background) and email support. Pro costs €29.99 and Business €59.99 a month.

### Is there a free trial or an annual price?

No. The paid plans have neither a trial nor an annual price: they're paid monthly, with no commitment. To try FillSell, the Free plan is all you need: test it on your real items, for as long as you like, without a credit card.

### Do I pay per marketplace or per listing posted?

Neither: you pay for a monthly plan. That plan counts the items written by AI: each new item is written once, then goes out to all your marketplaces at no extra count. A Lens scan counts as one listing on your plan. Each plan's volume is shown in the app before you buy, and your monthly counter stays in view; it resets on the anniversary date of your sign-up or of your subscription.

### Can I use FillSell from the UK or the US?

Not with UK or US marketplaces today. FillSell works with {{plateformes}}, with accounts based in France (vinted.fr for Vinted, ebay.fr for eBay). Vinted UK, eBay.co.uk and eBay.com aren't covered today. The app itself is in English and French, and prices are in euros.

### Why might the App Store show other prices?

FillSell's App Store page may still list older in-app purchases that are no longer part of today's offer. The current plans are the ones on this page: Free €0, Premium €12.99, Pro €29.99 and Business €59.99 a month. The price that counts is the one shown when you buy; in the stores, it can differ from the website's.

### How do I cancel my FillSell subscription?

Whenever you like: it runs until the end of the paid period, with no pro-rata refund. A subscription taken on the website is cancelled from the app. One taken through the App Store or Google Play is cancelled in the store: the app opens its subscription page for you.

### What happens to my subscription if I delete my account?

A subscription taken on the website is cancelled along with the account. One taken through the App Store or Google Play carries on until you cancel it in the store, so cancel it first. Your listings already live on the marketplaces stay live.
