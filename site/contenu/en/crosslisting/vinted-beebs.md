An item gathering dust on Vinted might be exactly what a Beebs parent is hunting for. Same photos, same price, a second crowd of buyers — and not a single word to retype. That's the whole point of this route: your wardrobe, two shop windows, one sale.

## Why put your Vinted listings on Beebs?

Because every extra shop window is another chance to sell. Beebs — branded “Beebs by Kiabi”, part of the Kiabi group since May 2024 — presents itself as the second-hand app “for the whole family” (our translation; Beebs publishes in French). By its own count, it reaches a market of “more than 2 million families” (Beebs help centre, text of 17 September 2026).

And Beebs is no longer just a baby-clothes app. Adult categories arrived in February 2024. Its catalogue rules (text of 5 January 2026) accept clothes, shoes and accessories for children aged 0 to 16, for men and for women, plus maternity wear, nursery gear, toys, children's books and the home: furniture, decor, linen, tableware and small kitchen appliances. In other words, most of a Vinted wardrobe fits right in.

The best part: listing is free for the seller on both. On Vinted and on Beebs alike, the buyer pays the service fee and postage. Two crowds, one card in your stock, nothing extra to pay to list.

## Can you import your Vinted wardrobe into Beebs?

In 2024, Beebs announced [on its blog](https://web.archive.org/web/20260301192436/https://www.beebs.app/blog/nouvelles-categories-adultes/) a “Synchro magique” feature (magic sync) to import listings published on other apps, without naming Vinted. As of 9 October 2026, its help centre has no article about that import, and we can't tell whether it still works today.

Without a tool, the move is manual: retype the title, copy the description, upload the photos again, hunt for the category, the size, the weight… item after item. Then keep an eye on two apps so the same piece never sells twice.

FillSell flips the problem around: your stock first, the marketplaces second. Your Vinted listings come into FillSell once, and each item goes to Beebs whenever you decide — or to any of the {{nb_plateformes}} marketplaces FillSell works with today: {{plateformes}}. ## Seven rules for selling on Vinted and Beebs at once

1. **One listing per item, per marketplace.** Vinted and Beebs both forbid offering the same item twice. In a batch, FillSell only sends each item where it isn't live yet.
2. **Never name the other marketplace in your listing.** Beebs refuses any reference to an outside website, and Vinted can hide a listing that promotes another platform. Your own text is never rewritten, so if your Vinted description mentions another site, fix that line before it goes to Beebs.
3. **Real photos only.** Beebs allows “no editing of the photo” (our translation), and Vinted doesn't accept retouched photos either. When you publish, send your photos as they are.
4. **Check Beebs accepts the item.** Underwear even when new, Temu and Shein items, smartphones: what's fine on Vinted isn't always fine on Beebs.
5. **The right status if you resell.** Both marketplaces keep private accounts for personal use. If you buy items to resell them, Vinted asks for a Vinted Pro account, and Beebs welcomes resellers under its Pro status.
6. **Give Beebs's moderation a moment.** Beebs checks new listings, so one can take a while to appear. A listing never seen online counts as a failed post, never as a sale.
7. **Shipping happens in the marketplace's own app.** After a Beebs order, you have 48 hours to accept it by generating the label, then 6 days to drop off the parcel.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can Beebs import my Vinted wardrobe?

Beebs presented an import of listings from other apps in 2024, without naming Vinted. Its help centre doesn't mention it as of 9 October 2026, and we don't know whether that import still works. With FillSell, your Vinted listings come into your stock with one tap on Sync, then go to Beebs whenever you choose.

### Do I have to retype my Vinted listings to put them on Beebs?

No. Photos, title, description, condition and price come from your card; FillSell matches the category, size, colour and parcel to Beebs. You read the Beebs version before it goes out, and the app only asks you something if a detail is missing.

### Can I sell on Vinted and Beebs from outside France?

Beebs handles payment and shipping in mainland France, Corsica and Belgium, and its help centre is in French. FillSell works today with Vinted France, so this route is for sellers based in France. The FillSell app itself is available in English, and Lens drafts your Beebs listings in French.

### Does my computer need to stay on?

Yes, for Vinted and Beebs: the Chrome extension posts, checks and removes your listings from your computer, with Chrome open. While listings are queued, it keeps the computer from going to sleep. Computer off? Nothing is lost: everything waits and goes out the next time Chrome opens.

### What happens when my item sells on Beebs?

FillSell sees that the Beebs listing is no longer online and asks you whether it sold there. Confirm with one tap: the sale is recorded and the proven Vinted copy is removed. A copy whose link isn't certain gets the question “Already sold?” before FillSell touches it.

### Does FillSell ask for my Vinted or Beebs password?

Never. You sign in to Vinted and Beebs yourself in Chrome, as usual, and the extension works inside those sessions. FillSell isn't affiliated with Vinted or Beebs.

### How much does it cost to move my listings from Vinted to Beebs?

Syncing is free, with no limit. Publishing a synced Vinted item on Beebs goes through the AI write-up, which counts as one item on your plan, and your own title and description are kept. ### Why doesn't my Beebs listing show up straight away?

Beebs checks new listings, so they can take a while to appear, and no official time frame is published. If a listing is never seen online, FillSell counts it as a failed post, never as a sale. Keep Beebs's rules in mind: unedited photos, no reference to another website, and an item its catalogue accepts.
