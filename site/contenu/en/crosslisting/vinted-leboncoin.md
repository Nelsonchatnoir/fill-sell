Two marketplaces. Two different crowds. One item, written once. Vinted shoppers come for clothes delivered in a parcel; Leboncoin, France's giant classifieds site, pulls in people hunting for everything from baby gear to bookshelves, many of them happy to collect in person. Put the same jacket in front of both and a whole new crowd can find it — without cutting the price or taking a single new photo.

## Can you sell the same item on Vinted and Leboncoin at once?

Yes, and it's one of the simplest ways to get more buyers in front of an item. The rules worth knowing sit on each side:

- **Leboncoin**: its help centre says you can't post the same listing twice on Leboncoin, and every listing must be written in French.
- **Vinted**: its help centre says the same item can't go up twice on Vinted. It also spells out what to do when something sells on another platform: “please hide or delete it from your Vinted listings instead of marking it as ‘Sold’” ([Vinted help, English version](https://www.vinted.ie/help/214)).

That last step is exactly what FillSell's extension does once a sale is recorded. What each marketplace's terms say about outside tools is a separate question: we quote the clauses word for word, with FillSell's safeguards, on our [account safety page (in French)](page:fr:securite-des-comptes).

Leboncoin is built for France. Its rules require listings written in French (a legal requirement under the French law of 4 August 1994), and its help centre says the site isn't designed for international use: from abroad you can browse and search, but you can't post a listing. So this route is for English speakers who live in France — which is where FillSell works today.

Three things worth knowing before your first Leboncoin listing:

- **Your words, untouched.** If you write the text yourself, or move a listing you wrote on Vinted, your title and description go out exactly as written. For Leboncoin, keep them in French.
- **No mention of other sites.** Lens is set never to name another website in a Leboncoin text: Leboncoin's rules forbid pointing buyers to another site, and Vinted can hide a listing whose description promotes another platform. Your own text isn't rewritten, so if your Vinted description says “Vinted” somewhere, fix that line before it heads to Leboncoin.

## Vinted to Leboncoin, or Leboncoin to Vinted: what moves and what adapts

Both directions work the same way, from your phone or from fillsell.app on your computer. An item live on Vinted gets a Leboncoin version; an item live on Leboncoin gets a Vinted version; a new item from a photo can be sent to both from the same screen.

- **What travels with the item**: its photos, your price, and the title and description (yours if you wrote them, Lens's French draft otherwise).
- **What adapts to each site**: category, size, colour, condition and parcel, each matched to that marketplace's own fields. The condition you chose is never upgraded on the way.
- **What happens when something's missing**: the app asks you a short question with ready-made answers. If no category fits, it asks you to pick one — it never greys a marketplace out because it couldn't decide.
- **Got a pile?** Batch publishing handles a whole selection at once, and each item only goes where it isn't live yet.

Two things to expect on the Leboncoin side: Leboncoin reviews new listings, so one can take a while to appear; and a listing that never shows up online counts as a failed post, never as a sale. On the Vinted side, FillSell works today with Vinted France (vinted.fr). Everything platform-specific is on the [Vinted](page:plateformes/vinted) and [Leboncoin (in French)](page:fr:plateformes/leboncoin) pages.

## Sold on Vinted, gone from Leboncoin — and the other way round

Selling on two sites has one real trap: the second buyer. FillSell closes it from both ends.

- **It sells on Vinted.** When Vinted marks your item sold, FillSell records the sale on its own and the extension removes the Leboncoin copy. You also get an email about that sale.
- **It sells on Leboncoin.** FillSell spots the sale and asks you to confirm it with one tap — a “sold” reading on Leboncoin never counts as a sale by itself. Once you confirm, the proven Vinted copy comes down, just as Vinted's own help asks.
- **Any doubt, it asks.** A copy is removed on its own only when FillSell can prove it's the same item: FillSell posted that copy, you linked the two, or the photos are identical. Otherwise you get the question “Already sold?”, with two answers: “Yes, remove it” or “No, it's another copy”. A “No” is never asked again.

A removal is final, and it needs your computer on with Chrome open. Computer off? The removal waits and goes out the next time Chrome opens. Every rule behind this is laid out on the [sales and removals page (in French)](page:fr:fonctions/ventes-et-retraits).

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I sell the same item on Vinted and Leboncoin at the same time?

Yes. Each site gets its own listing, posted through its own form. The rules to respect sit inside each site: no duplicate listing on Leboncoin, no duplicate item on Vinted. The real task is removing the other listing as soon as the item sells — Vinted's help asks you to hide or delete an item sold elsewhere. FillSell removes proven copies once a sale is recorded and asks “Already sold?” when it isn't sure.

### Do my Leboncoin listings have to be in French?

Yes. Leboncoin's rules require listings written in French, under a 1994 French law. The FillSell app works in English, and Lens drafts your Leboncoin and Vinted listings in French. If you rewrite the text yourself, it goes out exactly as you wrote it, so keep it in French for Leboncoin.

### Can I post on Leboncoin from outside France?

No. Leboncoin's help centre says posting a listing isn't available from abroad; you can still browse and search. FillSell works today with Vinted France and Leboncoin, so this route is for sellers based in France.

### What happens when my item sells on Leboncoin?

FillSell spots the sale and asks you to confirm it with one tap. Once confirmed, the Vinted copy is removed if FillSell can prove it's the same item; otherwise it asks “Already sold?” first. Leboncoin, for its part, gives you 48 hours after payment to confirm the item is still available, or the sale is cancelled.

### Does my computer need to be on?

Yes, for Vinted and Leboncoin: posting, removing and spotting sales go through the extension, so the computer must be on with Chrome open. You drive it from your phone. Computer off? Your listings wait and go out the next time Chrome opens.

### Is FillSell's reposting the same as a Vinted Boost or a Leboncoin bump?

No. Boost and the Leboncoin bump are paid options that keep your listing. FillSell reposts: it removes the listing and puts it back online, so it returns to the top of the results while its views and favourites start from zero. You can repost with one tap or, depending on your subscription, let automatic reposting run on {{republication}} in the time slot you choose. FillSell never buys a paid option for you.

### Do I need a pro account to resell on Vinted and Leboncoin?

If you buy items in order to resell them, both marketplaces say you need one. Vinted's catalogue rules reserve standard accounts for private, non-commercial use and point resellers to Vinted Pro. Leboncoin says a Pro account, free to create, is mandatory for professionals, and lists buying to resell and selling regularly in volume among its criteria.
